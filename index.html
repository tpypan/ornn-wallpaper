Ornn fluted glass frame picker

This renders ornn.com's hero shader live (seed 113, speed 0.15). The motion loops exactly every 8 min 43.6 s, and that one loop is all you can scrub. Saves one 5120×2880 sRGB PNG per colour, which is native on the Dell and fills the MacBook Air without upscaling.

Press S or ★ to keep frames you like here.
Navigate. Scroll over a preview to scrub (hold ⇧ for fine steps). ←/→ moves one frame (1/60 s), ⇧+arrow moves 1 s, and Space plays the motion at site speed. Click a frame in the strip to jump to it. Scrubbing past the end wraps to the start, because 523.6 s is the same image as 0 s.
Set as wallpaper. Renders both colours at 5120×2880 (128 passes, sRGB) and replaces ~/Pictures/Wallpapers/ornn-fluted-copper.png and ornn-fluted-gray.png in place. Your desktop then refreshes on its own. The filenames never change, so your wallpaper setting keeps working. The frame's time is stored inside each PNG, and the picker reopens on it next time.
Why one size. The Dell at "looks like 2560×1440" draws at exactly 5120×2880. The Air on "More Space" draws at 3420×2224, so "Fill Screen" shrinks the file to 0.77× and trims the sides to the dashed area. Neither display ever upscales it.
First time only. In System Settings → Wallpaper, choose ornn-fluted-copper.png (or gray) with "Fill Screen". After that, this button is all you need.
